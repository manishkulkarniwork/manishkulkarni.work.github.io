About

Producer-minded, detail-driven, and built around execution.

I work at the intersection of creative production and content strategy, helping projects move clearly from concept to final delivery.

Profile

I'm Manish Kulkarni, a Multimedia Producer and Content Strategist based in New York City and part of the founding team at Evolve Pictures LLC. My background spans live-streamed events, films, and digital-first content built for real audiences and real platforms.

Across four years of hands-on work, I've produced 40 live events, directed four films, and helped create a 68-reel Instagram series that generated 3.5 million views in 68 days.

What pulls me most is production itself: aligning people, timelines, creative priorities, technical workflows, and delivery standards so the work feels sharp and complete.

Production ownership

I like building the structure around the work: planning, resourcing, coordination, timing, and execution.

Creative fluency

I understand directing, shooting, editing, and post, which helps me communicate clearly across departments.

Strategic thinking

I think beyond the shoot itself and pay attention to audience, format, distribution, and performance.
